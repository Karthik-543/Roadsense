Sir,
Ministry has issued guidelines vide aforesaid letters for carrying out and uploading NSV
Survey data on online Centralised RAMSportal as well as PMIS Portal. In order to carry out the
NSV Survey on all stretches of NHs entrusted with State/UTs PWD, following guidelines may
be followed.
Zs
(A) Network Survey Vehicle (NSV) runs-
(i)
NSV survey needs to be carried out on all NH stretches entrusted with PWD.
In the
latest Contract, NSV Survey is included in the scope of Authority Engineer (AE)/ Independent
Engineer
(IE)/Project Management Consultancy
(PMC)/
Consultancy for Detailed
Project
Report (DPR). Wherever, NSV Surveyis not included in the scope of existing Contract, cost of
NSV Survey may be met out from contingency of any work as per Ministry Guideline dated
13.11.2019. However, in respect of EPC projects costing upto 300 cr. State PWDis acting

(ii)
For the NHs stretches entrusted with State Governments / UTs, NSV Survey needs to be
carried out for the stretches which are not covered under any ongoing work/defect liability

In such cases, cost

sanctioned from Ministry through RAMS Cell considering cost @ Rs. 4,500 per lane km perrun.
(iii)
Ministry vide Circular No. No. RW/G-23012/01/2019-W&A (Part-IV) dated 04.02.2021
has directed that data of NSV survey must be uploaded on the centralised RAMS portal and
link for the same has already been circulated to all ROs. It is reiterated that all NSV survey
data must be uploaded on centralised RAMSportalstrictly as per format (which is editable in
nature).

Guideline issued vide letter dated 13.11.2019

Project Management Consultancy (PMC)/ Consultancy for Detailed Project Report
(DPR).
